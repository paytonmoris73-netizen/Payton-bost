# Originality review (internal — not legal clearance)

This file records the internal design checks. **It is not a legal opinion.** Every design, name and the
MYTHLINGS brand must be reviewed by qualified IP counsel (trademark and copyright searches) before any commercial release.

## Process used
For each design: describe the silhouette and the most distinctive features; compare against well-known creature
franchises (the big creature-collecting series, major dragon designs in film and games, mobile pet/dragon games);
if a design seems close, change the distinctive features.

## Reference images supplied during development
A user-supplied image of a three-stage leafy creature evolution (marked "Copyright Glu Mobile") was used only to
understand the brief: *each evolution stage should be a genuinely different design*. No shapes, colours, proportions
or features were taken from it. Mosswarden deliberately avoids its key traits: no wood-grain body, no horns, no
leaf-crest along the back, no tufted lion tail.
